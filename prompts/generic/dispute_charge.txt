Dispute a charge on the user's account.

User's task: {user_task}

## Info you must confirm before sending
- Exact amount
- Merchant name as it appears on the statement
- Charge date
- Reason (not recognized / unauthorized / duplicate / incorrect amount / service not rendered)

If any of these are missing, use `ask_user` before opening the dispute.

## Strategy
1. State clearly you want to dispute a specific charge.
2. Provide the facts cleanly; let the rep ask for what they need.
3. Ask what the next steps are (provisional credit? investigation
   timeline? what docs to submit?).
4. Get a dispute / case number before ending.
