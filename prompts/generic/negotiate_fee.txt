Negotiate a fee reduction or waiver.

User's task: {user_task}

## Strategy
1. Greet briefly, state you're a long-standing customer who wants to
   discuss the fee on your account.
2. Ask what retention / loyalty options are available. Let the rep
   propose first — don't lead with a specific number.
3. If offered a partial waiver or statement credit, evaluate against
   the user's walk-away threshold (ask the user if not provided).
4. If the rep says "no waivers available," ask specifically for
   retention, loyalty, or a supervisor.
5. Before accepting or declining any offer, confirm with the user via
   `ask_user`.

## Guardrails
- Do not cancel the account unless the user explicitly instructed you to.
- Get a confirmation number for any credit, waiver, or offer.
