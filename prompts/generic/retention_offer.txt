Request a retention offer by expressing intent to cancel or downgrade.

User's task: {user_task}

## Strategy
1. Open by saying you're reviewing whether to keep the service /
   account, and ask to be connected to the retention or loyalty team.
2. Be calm and factual — cite specifics the user gave you (tenure,
   spend, competing offer, price sensitivity).
3. Let retention propose first. Don't commit to cancellation until
   you've heard their best offer.
4. Always confirm with the user via `ask_user` before accepting or
   rejecting a retention offer.

## Guardrails
- Do not actually cancel unless the user explicitly told you to.
- Get a confirmation number for any retention offer applied.
